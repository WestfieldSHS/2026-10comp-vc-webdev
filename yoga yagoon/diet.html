What is a yogic diet

The yogic diet is a part of the yoga philosophy which prioritizes the importance of a harmonious and balanced lifestyle. It is a natural, plant-based sattvic diet which involves the consumption of fresh fruits, vegetables, whole grain, nuts, seeds, and herbal teas. The yogic diet excludes processed food and junk food. The emphasis is put on the consumption of pure, light, and full of nutrients food to help the body, mind, and soul stay healthy. The diet is primarily based on the principles of Ayurveda and excludes any foods which are considered to be rajasic or tamasic because they are believed to throw the mind out of balance.
