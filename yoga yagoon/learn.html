Benefits of doing yoga

Studies indicate that although most people view yoga as a form of physical exercise, it is in fact both a physical activity and a mindful practice.↻ The physical elements of yoga are intended to challenge and enhance balance, flexibility, strength, and general health. Yet yoga can also serve as a meditation in motion. It brings about a physiological state that is the opposite of the fight-or-flight stress response. It halts the stress response, fostering a deeper sense of calm. Together, these two dimensions of yoga make it an excellent choice for people seeking to improve and support their overall health and wellbeing. Health professionals might even suggest yoga to patients as part of social prescribing: an approach that links people to groups and activities that address their health and wellbeing needs.
